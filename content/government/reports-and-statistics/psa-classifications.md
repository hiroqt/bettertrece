# Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) Standard Classification Systems

The Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA) provides standardized classification systems to organize, categorize, and report national and local statistics across geography, industries, occupations, education, crimes, and commodities.

---

## The 9 PSA Classification Systems

| System                                              | Acronym   | Base URL                                    | Documentation                                                  | Purpose                                                                 |
| :-------------------------------------------------- | :-------- | :------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Philippine Standard Geographic Code**             | `PSGC`    | `https://classification.psa.gov.ph/psgc`    | [PSGC Docs](https://psa.gov.ph/classifications-api/psgc)       | Standard coding for Regions, Provinces, Cities, and Barangays.          |
| **Philippine Standard Occupational Classification** | `PSOC`    | `https://classification.psa.gov.ph/psoc`    | [PSOC Docs](https://psa.gov.ph/classifications-api/psoc)       | Statistical classification of occupations in the labor force.           |
| **Individual Consumption According to Purpose**     | `PCOICOP` | `https://classification.psa.gov.ph/pcoicop` | [PCOICOP Docs](https://psa.gov.ph/classifications-api/pcoicop) | Household expenditure, CPI basket, and inflation tracking.              |
| **Philippine Standard Industrial Classification**   | `PSIC`    | `https://classification.psa.gov.ph/psic`    | [PSIC Docs](https://psa.gov.ph/classifications-api/psic)       | Economic activities and business line classifications.                  |
| **Philippine Central Product Classification**       | `PCPC`    | `https://classification.psa.gov.ph/pcpc`    | [PCPC Docs](https://psa.gov.ph/classifications-api/pcpc)       | Classification of goods, agricultural items, and services.              |
| **Philippine Standard Commodity Classification**    | `PSCC`    | `https://classification.psa.gov.ph/pscc`    | [PSCC Docs](https://psa.gov.ph/classifications-api/pscc)       | Standard commodity classifications for domestic & international trade.  |
| **Philippine Standard Classification of Education** | `PSCED`   | `https://classification.psa.gov.ph/psced`   | [PSCED Docs](https://psa.gov.ph/classifications-api/psced)     | Educational levels, degree programs, and vocational tracks.             |
| **Crime for Statistical Purposes**                  | `PSCCS`   | `https://classification.psa.gov.ph/psccs`   | [PSCCS Docs](https://psa.gov.ph/classifications-api/psccs)     | Criminal offense categories for police blotters and justice statistics. |
| **Tourism Statistical Classification System**       | `PTSCS`   | `https://classification.psa.gov.ph/ptscs`   | [PTSCS Docs](https://psa.gov.ph/classifications-api/ptscs)     | Tourism activities, accommodations, and cultural heritage assets.       |

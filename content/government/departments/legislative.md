## Legislative Mandate & Functions

The **Sangguniang Panlungsod** performs the following core constitutional functions:

1. **Ordinance & Resolution Enactment**: Formulating, deliberating, and enacting city tax ordinances, traffic regulations, zoning guidelines, environmental codes, and local policies.
2. **Budget Authorization**: Reviewing and approving the annual city executive budget and barangay annual budgets.
3. **Committee Inquiries & Hearings**: Conducting public hearings and committee investigations to protect consumer welfare, public peace, and community interests.
4. **Sectoral Representation**: Providing voice for the youth through the SK Federation and barangay communities through the Liga ng mga Barangay.

## Historical Significance of Trece Martires Barangays

Each of the 13 barangays is named in tribute to the **Thirteen Martyrs of Cavite (_Trece Martires de Cavite_)**, who were executed on September 12, 1896 for their dedication to Philippine independence and freedom.

- **City Capital / Seat of Government**: Brgy. San Agustin (Poblacion)
- **Largest Population**: Brgy. Hugo Perez
- **Fastest Growing Community**: Brgy. Aguado

## Governance & Barangay Public Services

Each Barangay Hall provides grassroots governance, community disputes resolution (Katarungang Pambarangay), local permits, and emergency response:

- **Barangay Clearance & Residency Certificates**
- **Community Mediation & Blotter Services (Lupon Tagapamayapa)**
- **Barangay Health Center & Immunization**
- **Solid Waste & Local Cleanliness Drives**
- **Barangay Disaster Risk Reduction & Management (BDRRMC)**

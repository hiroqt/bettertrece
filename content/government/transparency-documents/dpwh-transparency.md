## Strategic Infrastructure in Trece Martires City

As the provincial capital and central crossroads of Cavite, Trece Martires is host to critical national road networks and public facilities:

- **Governor's Drive (S01947LZ)**: Major 4-to-6 lane arterial highway connecting Cavite with SLEX and Carmona/Biñan corridors.
- **Trece Martires – Indang Road (S01948LZ)**: Upland agricultural trade corridor and tertiary road network.
- **Tejero – General Trias – Trece Martires Road (S01946LZ)**: Commercial and industrial access route connecting to EPZA and Cavite Economic Zone.
- **Evacuation Centers & Civic Facilities**: Multi-purpose calamity evacuation centers and health diagnostic facilities across Barangays San Agustin, Inocencio, and Hugo Perez.
- **River Basin Flood Management**: Slope protection and lined canals along the Rio Grande and Ilang-Ilang River catchments in Barangay Aguado and Barangay Osorio.

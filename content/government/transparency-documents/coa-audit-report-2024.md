## Key Audit Observations (CY 2024)

1. **Bank Reconciliation Statements (BRS)**: Submission delays and late journal adjustments across 9 Land Bank depository accounts totaling ₱182.04M.
2. **Property, Plant and Equipment (PPE)**: ₱15.64M discrepancy between records and physical count; 11 untitled land parcels; 5 road lots misclassified; 47 items without acquisition cost.
3. **Inventory Accounts (RPCI)**: ₱66.79M discrepancy between physical inventory and CAO general ledger; incomplete warehouse stock cards.
4. **Unrecorded Payables & CIP**: ₱45.84M in ongoing/completed works unrecorded at year-end (₱20.18M Accounts Payable, ₱25.66M CIP/PPE).
5. **Biological Assets**: Cattle livestock recorded at historical cost rather than fair market value; 12 deceased breeding cattle still carried in books.
6. **Dormant Accounts Payable**: ₱2.87M in obligations dormant for over 2 years without active claims awaiting reversion to the General Fund.
7. **Due to NGAs Trust Funds**: ₱2.60M in idle national government transfers older than 2 years under verification.
8. **Calamity Fund (LDRRMF) Expenses**: ₱2.41M for general supplies, training, and meals questioned for non-thematic DRRM alignment.
9. **Negative Slippage on Infrastructure Projects**: 7 local projects totaling ₱29.32M incurred negative slippages (7%–34.82%) without timely formal notice/catch-up plans, plus 45 contracts totaling ₱82.06M awarded to same contractor.
10. **Contract Time Extensions**: 2 infrastructure projects totaling ₱14.87M granted 52-day and 90-day time extensions without valid IRR grounds.
11. **Unserviceable Properties Disposal**: 160 unserviceable items worth ₱108.90M pending appraisal and public auction.
12. **LTO Vehicle Registrations**: 4 unregistered motor vehicles + 19 expired registrations being transferred to barangays via Deeds of Donation.
13. **Job Order (JO) Workforce**: 1,692 JO workers (₱63.90M wages) had non-specific contracts and generalized accomplishment reports.
14. **LDRRMF Monthly Reports**: Non-submission of monthly disaster fund utilization reports to COA.
15. **Gender and Development (GAD)**: ₱104.37M GAD fund had uneven utilization and lacked updated multi-year GAD agenda.

# Full Disclosure Policy (FDP) — City of Trece Martires

In compliance with the Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) Full Disclosure Policy (FDP) Memorandum Circular No. 2019-149, the City Government of Trece Martires posts its financial documents and procurement plans.

---

## 14 Mandatory Financial Documents

Local government units are required to publish the following quarterly and annual financial reports:

1. **Annual Budget Report**: Total revenue sources and approved expenditure allocations.
2. **Statement of Receipts and Expenditures (SRE)**: Actual collections vs targeted revenues.
3. **Quarterly Statement of Cash Flow**: Summary of cash receipts and cash disbursements.
4. **Special Education Fund (SEF) Utilization**: Allocation of SEF proceeds to public schools and scholarships.
5. **Trust Fund (TF) Utilization**: Utilization of earmarked trust fund transfers from national agencies.
6. **20% Component of IRA / NTA Utilization**: Annual investment plan implementation funded by the National Tax Allotment.
7. **Local Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Fund (LDRRMF) Utilization**: Calamity fund allocation and emergency procurement.
8. **Unliquidated Cash Advances**: Periodic reporting of cleared and active accountable officers' cash advances.
9. **Manpower Complement**: Summary of permanent, temporary, coterminous, and contractual personnel.
10. **Annual Procurement Plan (APP)**: Total planned procurement of goods, consulting, and civil works.
11. **Bid Results on Civil Works, Goods, and Consulting Services**: Published BAC notices of award and contractor lists.
12. **Supplemental Procurement Plan**: Any in-year approved procurement updates.
13. **Local Governance Performance Management System (LGPMS)**: Comprehensive city governance scorecards.
14. **City Council Ordinances on Appropriations**: Legislative enactment records for city budgets.

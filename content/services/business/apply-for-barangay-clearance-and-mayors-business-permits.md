# How to Set Up a Business Partnership — Trece Martires City

## Step 1: Register with SEC

**Prepare documents:**

- Partnership name (check via [SEC eSPARC](https://espac.sec.gov.ph/))
- Notarized Articles of Partnership
- IDs/TINs of partners, office address, capital structure

**File online** through [SEC eSPARC](https://espac.sec.gov.ph/) and pay fees.

**Output:** SEC Certificate of Recording + Approved Business Name

## Step 2: Apply for Business Permit (Mayor's Permit)

Go to **Trece Martires City BPLO** (Business Permits and Licensing Office at Trece Martires City Hall, Governor's Drive, San Agustin).

**Requirements:**

- Unified Business Permit Application Form
- Barangay Business Clearance (from the host Trece Martires barangay)
- SEC Certificate of Registration + Articles of Partnership (or DTI for Sole Proprietorship)
- Occupancy Permit (if owner) OR Lease Contract (if renting)
- Community Tax Certificate (Cedula / CTC)
- Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC) from Trece Martires BFP
- Sanitary Permit / Environmental Clearance

Apply in person at Trece Martires City Hall BOSS (Business One-Stop Shop) or coordinate with the City BPLO.

> Note: Inspections include zoning, sanitary, and Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP) safety checks.

## Step 3: Register with BIR

File at **BIR RDO 54B — Trece Martires City, Cavite** (Provincial Capitol Compound / Trece Martires).

- Fill out [BIR Form 1903](https://www.bir.gov.ph/images/1903%20Jan%202024.pdf) (Partnerships/Corporations) or Form 1901 (Individuals)
- Submit SEC/DTI Certificate + Articles of Partnership + valid IDs + Mayor's Permit
- Pay registration fees + Documentary Stamp Tax (DST)

**Within 30 days of receiving BIR Certificate:**

- Apply for Authority to Print Receipts (Form 1906) OR enroll in e-invoicing
- Register your Books of Accounts

## Step 4: Register as Employer (if hiring)

Enroll with:

- [SSS](https://www.sss.gov.ph/)
- [PhilHealth](https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/)
- [Pag-IBIG](https://www.pagibigfund.gov.ph/)

## Process Flow

1. SEC / DTI name check & registration via [SEC eSPARC](https://espac.sec.gov.ph/)
2. Secure Barangay Business Clearance + site/lease documents
3. Apply for Mayor's Business Permit at **Trece Martires City Hall BPLO**
4. File BIR Form at BIR RDO 54B Trece Martires → apply ATP → register books
5. Register mandatory statutory employer accounts (SSS, PhilHealth, Pag-IBIG)

## Document Checklist

- [ ] SEC Articles of Partnership / DTI Certificate
- [ ] Barangay Business Clearance (Trece Martires Barangay)
- [ ] Lease Contract OR Occupancy/Building docs
- [ ] Trece Martires BPLO Unified Application Form + CTC (Cedula)
- [ ] BIR Certificate of Registration (COR), ATP/Books

With these steps, your business in Trece Martires City will be fully compliant and ready to operate!

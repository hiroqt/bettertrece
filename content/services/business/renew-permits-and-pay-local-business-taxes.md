# Renew Permits and Pay Local Business Taxes — Trece Martires City

Use this guide to renew your **Mayor’s/Business Permit** and **Local Business Taxes (LBT)** with the City Government of Trece Martires.

---

## Who Must Renew

- All businesses operating in Trece Martires City across any of the 13 barangays: **sole proprietors, partnerships, corporations, NGOs, cooperatives, and retail/service establishments**.
- Renewal applies to **main offices and every branch/stall** operating within the city.

---

## When to Renew

- **Annual renewal period:** **January 1 to January 20** of every year (or extended deadlines set by City Ordinance).
- **Payments:** either **annually** in full (often with early bird prompt-payment discounts if provided by city ordinance) or **quarterly** (January 20, April 20, July 20, October 20).
- Late filings incur **surcharges (up to 25%) and monthly interest (2%)**.

---

## Where to Renew

- **Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS)**  
  Trece Martires City Hall, Governor's Drive, San Agustin, Trece Martires City  
  BPLO Hotline: (046) 419-0268 / (046) 419-1065

---

## What You’ll Need (Typical Requirements)

1. **Previous Year Documents**
   - Previous Year **Mayor's / Business Permit** and Official Receipts (OR)
   - Community Tax Certificate (Cedula) of the current year

2. **Gross Sales Declaration**
   - Sworn Declaration of Gross Sales / Receipts of the preceding year
   - VAT / Percentage Tax Returns (BIR Form 2550M/Q / 2551Q) or Audited Financial Statements

3. **Regulatory Clearances**
   - **Barangay Business Clearance** for the current year
   - **Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (FSIC)** from Trece Martires BFP
   - **Sanitary Permit / Health Cards** from Trece Martires City Health Office
   - **CENRO / Solid Waste Clearance** from Trece Martires CENRO

---

## Step-by-Step Process (Trece Martires BOSS)

1. **Submission & Assessment:** Present documents at Trece Martires City BPLO for document validation and assessment of local business taxes and regulatory fees.
2. **Clearances Verification:** Settle BFP Fire Safety inspection fees and Sanitary/Health fees.
3. **Payment:** Pay at the City Treasurer's Office cashier windows.
4. **Permit Release:** Claim your validated Mayor's Permit, business plate/sticker, and FSIC/Sanitary permit.
5. **Display:** Display the Mayor's Permit and Official Receipt prominently at your place of business.

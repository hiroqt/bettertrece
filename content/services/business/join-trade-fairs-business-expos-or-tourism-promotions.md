# Participate in Local Trade Fairs, Business Expos, and Tourism Promotion Activities — Trece Martires City

Showcase your products and services, meet buyers, and boost your brand by joining **city- and province-led trade fairs, business expos, and tourism promotions in Trece Martires City, Cavite**.

---

## Who Can Join

- **MSMEs** (sole proprietors, partnerships, corporations, cooperatives)
- **Tourism & Hospitality enterprises** (accommodations, dining, events, travel services)
- **Cultural creators & Artisans** (handicrafts, Cavite native delicacies, fashion, agriculture, creative tech)
- **LGU-accredited community groups** (livelihood associations, women/youth/farmers' cooperatives)

**Priority is often given** to registered local businesses in Trece Martires City, OTOP (One Town, One Product) branded items, and active livelihood groups.

---

## Event Types You’ll See

- **Trece Martires City Fiesta & Trece Martires Day (May 24)** – annual cityhood and historical celebrations
- **Cavite Provincial Trade Fairs** – regional buyers' meets, B2B networking, DTI OTOP Hub showcases
- **Agri-Tourism & Livelihood Bazaars** – local farm produce, native delicacies, and MSME products
- **Barangay Trade Fairs & Night Markets** – organized in central barangays during holidays and festivals

---

## How to Participate (Step-by-Step)

1. **Prepare eligibility documents**
   - DTI/SEC/CDA registration, BIR 2303
   - Mayor’s/Business Permit (Trece Martires City)
   - Product compliance (FDA/LTO if food/cosmetics)

2. **Enroll or pre-register**
   - Submit an **Application/Intent Form** with business profile, product list, and booth requirements.
   - Coordinate with the **DTI Negosyo Center Trece Martires** or the City Information Office.

3. **Assessment & confirmation**
   - Organizers screen for product quality, safety, and local origin.
   - Once accepted, confirm booth assignments and guidelines.

4. **Pre-event readiness**
   - **Collateral:** banners, price cards, brochures, QR codes
   - **Packaging & labeling:** clean, compliant, and clear ingredients
   - **Payment options:** Cash + GCash / Maya QR codes

5. **Event days**
   - Follow venue rules, fire safety, and waste segregation.

6. **Post-event follow-up**
   - Keep customer contacts, fulfill repeat orders, and evaluate sales.

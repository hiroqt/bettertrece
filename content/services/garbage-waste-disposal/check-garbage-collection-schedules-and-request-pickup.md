# Check Garbage Collection Schedules and Request Pickup — Trece Martires City

The City Government of Trece Martires manages **regular garbage collection** through the **City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO)** and barangay solid waste committees. Residents and business owners are encouraged to follow the schedule and proper segregation rules across all 13 barangays.

---

## 1) Regular Garbage Collection

**How it works**

- Collection schedules operate across the 13 barangays (San Agustin, Luciano, De Ocampo, Inocencio, Conchu, Hugo Perez, Cabuco, Perez, Aguado, Gregorio, Lallana, Osorio, Lapidario).
- City collection trucks cover **household waste**, **biodegradable**, and **residual/non-recyclable** garbage according to the barangay route.
- Segregation at source is **mandatory** — separate biodegradable, recyclable, residual, and special/hazardous waste.

**Where to check schedules**

1. **Barangay Hall** — each barangay hall posts its designated truck collection days and routes.
2. **City Information & CENRO Announcements** — check city advisories for holiday or weather-related schedule changes.

> Tip: Post the collection calendar on your fridge or gate to remind the household which days to take bins out.

---

## 2) Special Garbage Pickup (Bulk or Non-Routine Waste)

For **bulk waste** (furniture, large appliances, pruned tree branches), collection is not part of the regular household route.

**How to request**

1. Visit your **Barangay Hall** or contact the **Barangay Environment Committee**.
2. Provide details: type of waste, estimated volume, and address.
3. The barangay coordinates with **Trece Martires CENRO** for scheduling a **special truck pickup**.

## 4) Quick Tips for Residents

- Put garbage out **only on collection day**, early in the morning.
- Use durable, covered containers to prevent animal scattering.
- For recyclables, coordinate with your **Barangay Materials Recovery Facility (MRF)**.
- Avoid burning waste (strictly prohibited under RA 9003 / Clean Air Act).

---

### Key Reminder

Following your barangay’s **garbage collection schedule** and properly requesting **special pickups** keeps Trece Martires City clean, green, and flood-free.

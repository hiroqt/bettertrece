# Guidelines for Proper Waste Segregation and Disposal — Trece Martires City

Proper waste segregation is **mandatory** in Trece Martires City under **Republic Act 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act)** and local city ordinances. Following these rules helps keep the city clean, prevents flooding, and protects public health across all 13 barangays.

---

## 1) Waste Categories You Must Separate

| Category                                         | Examples                                                                         | How to Dispose                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Biodegradable (Nabubulok)**                    | Food scraps, vegetable/fruit peelings, garden waste, dry leaves                  | Place in green container/biodegradable bin; collected by trucks; can also be composted at home/barangay MRF        |
| **Recyclable (Di-nabubulok na pwede i-recycle)** | Plastic bottles, glass, tin/aluminum cans, paper, cartons, scrap metal           | Clean and dry before disposal; place in blue container/recyclable bin; bring to junkshops or barangay MRF          |
| **Residual (Di-nabubulok / Basura)**             | Used diapers, styrofoam, plastic wrappers, sachets, contaminated paper, ceramics | Place in black container/residual bin; collected by trucks and sent to sanitary landfill                           |
| **Special/Hazardous Waste**                      | Batteries, light bulbs, e-waste, paint cans, chemicals, medical waste            | Do **not** mix with regular waste; bring to barangay/CENRO special collection drives or accredited drop-off points |

---

## 2) General Rules for Segregation

- **Always use labeled bins** or color-coded bags (green = biodegradable, blue = recyclable, black = residual).
- **Rinse recyclables** to avoid pests and foul odor.
- **Do not burn waste** — open burning is strictly prohibited under RA 9003 and the Clean Air Act.
- **Put garbage out only on your barangay’s collection schedule** to avoid scattered trash and clogged drainage.
- For **bulk items (furniture, appliances)** → request a **special pickup** through your Barangay Hall or Trece Martires CENRO.
- **Construction debris** must be hauled by private accredited haulers; not allowed in regular household collection.

---

## 3) Barangay Materials Recovery Facilities (MRFs)

- Each of the 13 barangays in Trece Martires operates an **MRF** to manage recyclables and compostables.
- Residents can drop off segregated plastics, paper, metals, and garden waste.

---

## 4) For Businesses & Establishments

- Must maintain **segregated waste bins** accessible to customers and staff.
- Required to adhere to solid waste management clearances during annual Mayor's Permit renewals at BPLO.
- Keep disposal receipts/hauler contracts for inspection by Trece Martires CENRO.

---

## 5) Penalties for Non-Compliance

- **Improper waste disposal or open dumping** will result in fines and community service under city ordinances and RA 9003.
- Repeated violations may lead to **business permit suspension** for commercial establishments.

---

## 6) Quick Tips for Households

- Keep **3–4 labeled bins** at home (biodegradable, recyclable, residual, hazardous).
- Reduce waste by bringing reusable eco-bags when shopping at Trece Martires Public Market and local stores.
- Compost kitchen waste for garden soil enrichment.

# Access Educational Support Programs from the LGU — Trece Martires City

Below are **city-led** and **LGU-facilitated** programs you can use for study support: free school supplies, student employment (SPES) via PESO Trece Martires, city college scholarships, and DSWD/CSWD educational assistance.

---

## 1) Free School Supplies & Student Assistance (SEF-funded)

**What it is**

- The City of Trece Martires provides **free school supplies** and educational kits to public school learners through the **Special Education Fund (SEF)**, distributed annually to elementary and high school students.

**How to access**

1. Watch your school’s advisory and **DepEd Trece Martires** / SDO Cavite memos for **distribution schedules**.
2. Learners and parents claim supplies at the public school on the designated distribution date.

---

## 2) Student Employment (SPES) via PESO Trece Martires

**What it is**

- The **Special Program for the Employment of Students (SPES)** enables deserving **students and out-of-school youth (aged 15–30)** in Trece Martires City to work during vacation and school breaks to earn tuition support and gain government work experience.
- Implemented locally through the **Public Employment Service Office (PESO)** in partnership with DOLE.

**How to access**

1. Coordinate with **PESO Trece Martires City** at City Hall for **SPES application windows**.
2. Prepare student ID, enrollment form/grades, and proof of family income/indigency.
3. Submit requirements at the PESO office; attend orientation when shortlisted.

---

## 3) Trece Martires City College Scholarships & Youth Grants

**What it is**

- The City Government of Trece Martires offers **city educational grants and scholarship programs** for qualified college and vocational students residing in any of the 13 barangays.
- Higher education access is also supported through **Cavite State University (CvSU) — Trece Martires City Campus**.

**How to access**

1. Monitor the **Trece Martires City Government Facebook Page** and the **City Mayor's Office** for scholarship intake announcements.
2. Submit Certificate of Residency, Certificate of Grades (GWA), and enrollment confirmation.

---

## 4) Educational Assistance (CSWD & DSWD AICS)

**What it is**

- **Emergency educational assistance** for students from indigent families, solo parents, or crisis-affected households, managed by the **City Social Welfare and Development (CSWD)** office and DSWD CALABARZON (Region IV-A).

**How to access**

1. Visit the **CSWD Office** at Trece Martires City Hall Compound.
2. Present Barangay Certificate of Indigency, valid ID, and School Registration / Assessment form.

---

## Quick Directory

- **Trece Martires City Mayor's Office** — scholarship applications & city educational aid
- **PESO Trece Martires City** — SPES student employment, youth job fairs & career training
- **City Social Welfare & Development (CSWD)** — educational financial assistance (AICS)
- **DepEd SDO Trece Martires** — public school policies, SEF supplies, ALS programs

---

## Tips to Maximize Support

- Keep a document folder with **PSA Birth Certificate**, **Barangay Residency / Indigency**, **School ID / COE**, **Report Cards**, and **2x2 ID photos** ready.
- Turn on notifications for the **City Government of Trece Martires** official page so you don’t miss application deadlines.

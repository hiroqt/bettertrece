# Learn About Supplementary Activities for Schools in Your Area — Trece Martires City

This guide shows where to find **clubs, student organizations, competitions, service groups, and enrichment programs** available to learners in Trece Martires City, Cavite.

---

## Where to Look First (Local)

1. **Your school’s official portal or social media page** — clubs post enlistment and tryout dates there.
2. **DepEd Division of Trece Martires City / SDO Cavite** — division-wide contests, leadership trainings, and press conferences.
3. **Cavite State University (CvSU) — Trece Martires City Campus** — university extension programs, academic seminars, and collegiate organizations.
4. **City Youth & Sports Development Office** — sports clinics, inter-school tournaments, and civic youth summits.

## Academic & Interest Clubs

- **STEM & Robotics Clubs** — science fairs, investigatory math & science projects, coding workshops
- **Arts, Theater & Music Guilds** — chorale, drum and lyre corps, cultural dance troupes, visual arts
- **Sports Teams & Athletics** — basketball, volleyball, athletics, badminton representing Trece Martires City in Cavite Provincial Meets / CLRAA
- **Alternative Learning System (ALS)** — community learning centers across Trece Martires barangays for out-of-school youth

---

## How to Join (Quick Steps)

1. **Check school announcements** at the start of the school year for Club Recruitment Week.
2. **Prepare simple requirements** (student ID, parental consent).
3. **Attend orientation** and club assemblies.
4. **Participate actively** in division competitions, community outreach, and school events.
